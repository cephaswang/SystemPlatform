AVEVA Historian is a high-speed industrial process database, that acquires and stores data, for comprehensive diagnostics and troubleshooting of process anomalies. Historian data can be intuitively accessed through AVEVA Operations Management Interface, the visualization for AVEVA System Platform, and separately accessible with the AVEVA Historian Client data analysis tools.
